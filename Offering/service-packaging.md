# Service Packaging - First Cut (Gerry's Three Questions)

> Drafted 28 Jul 2026 in response to Gerry's ask: how do we describe this service to
> potential clients, what do we arm a salesperson with, and why should a client choose
> us. Review chain: Philip first, then Gerry. This later becomes the practice one-pager
> and sales deck.

## 1. How we describe the service

**One-liner:** For companies that know AI matters but do not know where to start: a
fixed-fee, six to eight week study that maps how the business actually runs, identifies
where process re-engineering, automation, and AI can grow revenue per employee, and
delivers a stack-ranked, costed roadmap that can be executed immediately, in-house or
with us.

**The pitch in three sentences:** Most companies are stuck between AI pilots and real
impact, and the stall is organizational, not technological. We study the business the
way engineers do, process by process, and quantify where the leverage is. In six to
eight weeks the leadership team gets a prioritized plan with numbers they can defend to
their board.

**The method in one breath:** re-engineer the process first, automate what is
rule-based, apply AI where it genuinely changes the economics; every opportunity
costed, plotted on return vs effort, and stack-ranked.

## 2. What the salesperson carries

1. **The offer card:** duration, fixed fee, named deliverables (readiness scorecard,
   opportunity portfolio on the return vs effort matrix, initiative outlines with
   build / buy / partner options, first-90-days plan, executive readout).
2. **A sample report on a fictional company.** The single most convincing artifact for
   a "show me what I get" buyer. Next collateral investment.
3. **Three door-opener questions:**
   - What did your last AI pilot change in the P&L?
   - If your team doubled tomorrow at the same cost, where would you point it?
   - Who in your company can draw how a customer order actually flows end to end?
4. **Objection answers, written down:**
   - *Why not a big firm?* Same study, a fraction of the fee, and a plan you can build
     rather than a strategy deck.
   - *You will just tell us to hire you.* Fixed fee independent of any build decision;
     every recommendation carries build / buy / partner options; the client owns
     everything.
   - *Our data is not ready.* Data readiness is one of the things the study assesses;
     it is not an entry ticket.
   - *We can do this in-house.* Individual productivity, yes. Department-level
     re-architecture needs dedicated structure from outside the daily grind, which is
     exactly what internal teams do not have time for.
5. **Qualification profile:** mid-market (roughly 100 to 500 employees), operations- or
   GTM-heavy, board or CEO pressure on AI, pilots that have not moved the P&L.

## 3. Why choose us

1. **We build what we recommend.** Estimates come from people who deliver these
   systems, so the roadmap is buildable, not theoretical. We live with our own advice.
2. **Engineering depth at mid-market price.** A comparable study from the big firms
   runs several times our fee and stops at strategy altitude. We go to workflow level,
   where the value actually is.
3. **Independence by design.** Fixed fee, no obligation to build with us, all
   artifacts owned by the client. If the right answer is the in-house team or another
   vendor, the report says so.
