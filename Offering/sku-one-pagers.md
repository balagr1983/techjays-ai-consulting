## SKU 1: AI Opportunity Study

**Buyer:** CEO/COO of a mid-market company (roughly USD 100 million to USD 1,000 million
revenue) whose board is asking about AI, who does not want to pay big-firm fees for an
abstract strategy deck.

**Problem it solves:** "We know AI matters. We do not know where it pays, what it costs,
or where to start, and we are being pushed to act."

**What we do (8 weeks):**
- Weeks 1-2: Discovery - executive kickoff, 10 to 15 stakeholder interviews, systems and
  data walkthrough, process observation.
- Weeks 3-4: Opportunity inventory - candidate AI opportunities across revenue, margin,
  and client experience; workflow mapping of the affected processes.
- Weeks 5-6: Scoring - ROI and cost estimates per opportunity, framework scoring, the
  Techjays 2x2, stack-ranking; high-level architecture sketch per top opportunity.
- Week 7: Roadmap - sequenced 90-day / 6-month / 12-month plan with business case.
- Week 8: Executive readout, report handover, optional board presentation.

**Deliverables:** scored readiness assessment (6 dimensions), opportunity portfolio on
the 2x2, stack-ranked costed roadmap, architecture sketches for top opportunities,
KPI/measurement plan, one-page executive summary. 30 to 40 page report.

**Team:** engagement lead + AI solution architect + business analyst (fractional
specialists as needed).

**Price:** USD 100,000 to USD 150,000 fixed fee.

**Why Techjays:** a quarter of big-firm fees; we build what we recommend (and have to
live with our own advice); every recommendation comes with build / buy / partner options
and the client owns everything.

**Follow-on:** fixed-bid delivery proposal for any roadmap item, priced separately.

## SKU 2: AI Pre-Readiness Study (Operations Deep Map)

**Buyer:** operations-heavy company (manufacturing, logistics, services ops) that knows
transformation is coming and wants its processes mapped to the tiniest detail before
committing.

**Problem it solves:** "Nobody, including us, actually knows how our operations work
end to end. We cannot automate what we have not mapped."

**What we do (8 weeks):** process-level mapping of the target operation (every workflow,
handoff, system touch, data artifact, exception path), readiness scoring per process,
target-state design showing where AI/automation changes each workflow, prioritized
transformation sequence.

**Deliverables:** the operations map (current state), AI-enabled target-state design,
per-process readiness scores, gap analysis, sequenced transformation roadmap with cost
estimates, executive summary.

**Team:** engagement lead + 2 business/process analysts + AI solution architect
(part-time).

**Price:** around USD 150,000 fixed fee.

**Why Techjays:** this is our DND (Design and Discovery) muscle re-narrated as a
standalone consulting product; workflow-level depth is the thing big-firm studies skip
and the highest-leverage factor for AI value.

**Follow-on:** SKU 1-style prioritization on top of the map, or direct fixed-bid build.

## SKU 0 (proposed addition): AI Opportunity Scan

**What:** 1 to 2 day paid executive briefing - half-day leadership workshop, rapid
opportunity scan against the framework, a short readout memo with the top 3 candidate
opportunities.

**Price:** USD 7,500 to USD 15,000 (market analog: Slalom sells a 4-hour workshop at
about USD 6,800 list).

**Why it exists:** low-friction entry that converts to SKU 1. A paid taster beats a free
one: it qualifies the buyer and values our time. Decision for Philip: worth having at
launch, or does it dilute focus?
