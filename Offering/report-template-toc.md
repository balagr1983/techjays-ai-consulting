# Assessment Report - Table of Contents Mock v0.1

> The market-standard deliverable set (verified across PwC, Kriv, Storm Reply) arranged
> for the Techjays report. Target length 30 to 40 pages. This becomes the skeleton of the
> sample/demo report in Phase 2.

1. **Executive Summary** (1 page)
   - Headline readiness score, top 3 opportunities, the one-paragraph recommendation.
2. **Why This Study** (1 page)
   - Client context, the three-value frame (revenue, margin, client experience), scope
     and method summary.
3. **Readiness Scorecard** (2-3 pages)
   - Six-dimension scores with level descriptions; benchmark context where available;
     what moves each score one level.
4. **How Your Operation Actually Works** (4-6 pages)
   - Workflow maps of the processes studied; pain points and friction observed; the
     "nobody had this on one page before" section. Differentiator section.
5. **Opportunity Portfolio** (4-6 pages)
   - Full inventory; the Techjays 2x2 (ROI vs cost); stack-ranked list with one-line
     rationale each.
6. **Top Opportunity Deep Dives** (2-3 pages each, top 3 to 5)
   - Current vs target workflow, high-level architecture, ROI model and assumptions,
     cost estimate, build / buy / partner options, risks.
7. **Roadmap and Business Case** (2-3 pages)
   - 90-day / 6-month / 12-month sequence; dependencies; cumulative investment vs
     cumulative value curve.
8. **Governance, Risk and Trust** (2 pages)
   - Responsible AI posture, compliance mapping where relevant, agent governance
     recommendation.
9. **Measurement Plan** (1-2 pages)
   - KPIs per initiative, baseline values, tracking cadence. (Fewer than 1 in 5
     companies do this; cheap differentiation.)
10. **Appendices**
    - Interview list, systems and data reviewed, method notes, framework level
      definitions, assumptions register.

House rules for the report: no em dashes; money as USD figures; every number traceable
to an interview, a document, or a stated assumption; every recommendation carries a
non-Techjays option where one credibly exists.
