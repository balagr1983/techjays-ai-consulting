# Roadmap - Standing Up the Techjays AI Consulting Practice

> v0.1 draft, 17 Jul 2026. Bala's working draft for the follow-up call with Philip.
> Evidence base: `Research/` (competitive teardown, pricing, frameworks, buyer signals).
> Dates are placeholders until Bala and Philip lock the plan.

## Guiding conclusions from the research

1. The purchasable market format for our offering already exists: **fixed-fee, fixed
   4 to 8 week, deliverable-rich assessment** ending in a stack-ranked, costed roadmap.
   We are not inventing a category, we are entering a recognized one at a better price
   point with build capability behind it.
2. **A named proprietary framework is table stakes** (PwC scores 12 domains, Accenture 8
   dimensions, Thoughtworks 6). Techjays needs its own named framework; the 2x2
   ROI-vs-cost stack-rank is our signature output.
3. Differentiation holds up: mid-market price (roughly a quarter of big-firm fees),
   skin in the game (we build what we recommend), workflow-level depth (the DND muscle,
   and the highest-leverage gap per McKinsey's own data). But West Monroe and Accenture
   already march on the same "strategy without execution is empty" argument, so speed
   matters.

## Phase 1 - Offering definition (target: Aug 2026)

- Define the **Techjays assessment framework**: name it, fix the scored dimensions
  (recommended core: strategy, data, technology, talent, governance/risk, operating
  model/workflow; differentiate by scoring workflows at process level, not org level).
- Define the two SKUs from the 16 Jul call:
  1. **AI Opportunity Study** - about 8 weeks, USD 100,000 to USD 150,000: opportunity
     inventory, 2x2 ROI-vs-cost matrix, stack-ranked costed roadmap.
  2. **AI Pre-Readiness Study** - about 8 weeks, around USD 150,000: process-level
     mapping of operations to the tiniest detail, readiness scoring, target-state design.
  Consider a third, small SKU as the funnel opener (market pattern: Slalom's paid 4-hour
  workshop, Thoughtworks' free scan): e.g. a 1 to 2 day paid **AI Opportunity Scan**
  briefing for CEOs.
- Design the report template (market standard: scored gap analysis, benchmarking,
  prioritized use-case portfolio, architecture views, ROI/cost estimates, 90-day /
  6-month / 12-month roadmap, governance section, one-page executive summary; 30 to 40
  pages).
- Decide the bias-objection answer in writing (e.g. fixed-fee study priced independently
  of build; recommendations include buy/partner options, not only build).
- Staffing model per engagement (market benchmark: boutiques staff 1 to 3 people).
- Exit criteria: framework documented; both SKU one-pagers drafted; sample report
  skeleton exists.

## Phase 2 - Collateral and packaging (target: Sep 2026)

- Sales deck + one-pager per SKU (Techjays doc standards).
- A **sample/demo assessment report** on a fictional company - the single most convincing
  sales artifact for a "show me what I get" buyer.
- Rebrand/re-narrate the existing DND materials so delivery and consulting tell one story.
- Decide channels: direct (Philip's CEO network first), plus evaluate AWS/Azure
  marketplace listings (verified market pattern; partner funding can subsidize the
  client's cost).
- Exit criteria: a CEO can be walked from cold intro to signed SoW with existing
  materials.

## Phase 3 - Pilot engagements (target: Q4 2026)

- 1 or 2 friendly existing clients at pilot pricing (candidates from Philip's book; the
  enterprise-readiness opportunity discussed on 16 Jul may be the natural first).
- Deliberately over-invest; harvest a case study and testimonial; measure
  assessment-to-build conversion.
- Refine framework, report template, and effort model from actuals.
- Exit criteria: one referenceable case study; validated margin at list price.

## Phase 4 - Team (parallel, as demand firms up)

- Practice lead + 1 or 2 consultants from Bala's network pipeline; delivery consultants
  can be fractional from existing Techjays teams initially.
- Exit criteria: practice can run two concurrent engagements without pulling Philip in.

## Phase 5 - Launch and scale (target: from Q1 2027)

- Publish the case study; announce the practice; systematic cross-sell into every DND
  and delivery account.
- Revisit pricing with pilot data; decide whether an outcome-based component makes sense
  (top firms are moving there: ~25% of McKinsey fees are outcome-based).
- Quarterly refresh of the competitive teardown (offering pages change fast).
