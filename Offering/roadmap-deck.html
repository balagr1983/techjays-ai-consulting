Three differentiators, one honest caveat.

Skin in the game

with written safeguards

We build what we recommend, and the method protects the advice: fixed fee independent of any build decision, every recommendation carries build / buy / partner / do-nothing options, the client owns every deliverable.

Workflow depth

not org-level abstraction

Big-firm studies score the organization. We map the actual processes, because that is where AI value hides and that is the muscle our DND teams already have.

Accenture launched a Carnegie Mellon co-branded maturity model in June 2026 attacking "strategy without engineering rigor", and West Monroe already markets integrated strategy plus build.
