# Team Plan - Staffing the AI Consulting Practice

> v0.1 draft, 21 Jul 2026. Working document for internal scouting and network hiring.
> Principle: fractional-first. Nobody leaves billable delivery work until the pilot
> proves the practice margin.

## The insight

Techjays people who run Design and Discovery (DND) engagements are already doing
quasi-consulting: studying current systems, reimagining them, facilitating workshops,
writing scoped recommendations. The internal search is not "find consultants", it is
"find the delivery people who already show consulting traits on DND phases".

## Roles per engagement (boutique staffing model: 1 to 3 people)

| Role | Owns | Profile traits |
|------|------|----------------|
| Engagement lead | Client relationship, exec interviews, readout, commercial framing | Thinks in revenue/margin/client-experience terms; comfortable with CEOs; runs a room |
| AI solution architect | Feasibility, architecture sketches, build/buy/partner calls, cost estimates | Real build credibility (the skin-in-the-game proof); can say "no, do not build that" |
| Business/process analyst | Stakeholder interviews, workflow mapping, the report itself | Structured elicitation, facilitation, writing quality; the product is a document |

Practice-level at launch: Bala (practice operations + engagement lead), Philip (selling
and CEO access), fractional delivery pod, executive sponsor (see politics note).

## Screening criteria for internal candidates

1. Has run or materially improved a DND / discovery phase.
2. Writing quality: can produce client-grade documents without heavy editing.
3. Facilitation: has run workshops or client working sessions, not just attended.
4. Business-value fluency: frames work in outcomes, not features.
5. Presence: credible in front of a senior client audience.
6. Appetite: actually wants advisory work (some great builders do not).

## Politics and sequencing

- **Dharma (Chief AI Officer):** the practice overlaps his domain. Get his blessing
  early; ideally he is the internal executive sponsor. Added to the Philip decision
  list: where does the practice sit organizationally?
- Do not destabilize billable projects; the pilot engagement is deliberately staffed
  fractionally to test both the offering and the people.
- The pilot doubles as the tryout: whoever shines on the pilot becomes the practice core.

## How to extend the map beyond the NSR slice

1. Ask 2 or 3 delivery leads: "who on your teams is the best in discovery phases and in
   front of clients?" (frame as a capability inventory, not a poaching run).
2. Collect DND artifacts from other accounts; the best-written ones point to authors.
3. Bala shares any staff/org list here and we screen against the six criteria.
