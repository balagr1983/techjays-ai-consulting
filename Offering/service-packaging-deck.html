How it works

Re-engineer first. Automate next. AI where it changes the economics.

Every process goes through the same three layers, in order, and every opportunity comes out costed, plotted on return vs effort, and stack-ranked.

Layer 1
Re-engineer
Simplify the process before any technology is added.
Layer 2
Automate
Rules, integrations, and workflow automation where certainty is enough.
Layer 3
Apply AI
Including agents, where judgment, language, and 24/7 operation genuinely pay.
Output
Prioritize
Every opportunity costed and stack-ranked on return vs effort.

What it is

  • A quantified operating plan
  • A scored assessment and an opportunity portfolio on a return vs effort matrix
  • A sequenced roadmap with a concrete first 90 days

What it is not

  • A strategy deck
  • A technology audit
  • A staffing pitch

02

Engineering depth at a mid-market price

A comparable study from the big firms runs several times our fee and stops at strategy altitude. We work at workflow level: the actual steps, handoffs, and systems, where the value hides.
