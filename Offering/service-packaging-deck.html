1 · The service

How we describe the service

In one line

A fixed-fee, six to eight week study that turns "we know AI matters but not where to start" into a costed, prioritized roadmap.

We map how the business actually runs, identify where process re-engineering, automation, and AI can grow revenue per employee, and deliver a stack-ranked plan that can be executed immediately, in-house or with us.

The pitch

  1. Most companies are stuck between AI pilots and real impact, and the stall is organizational, not technological.
  2. We study the business the way engineers do, process by process, and quantify where the leverage is.
  3. In six to eight weeks the leadership team gets a prioritized plan with numbers they can defend to their board.

MIT NANDA, The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025: about 95% of enterprise gen AI pilots show little to no measurable P&L impact, attributed to workflow integration, not model quality.  ·  McKinsey, The State of AI 2025: workflow redesign is the strongest of 25 factors driving AI's bottom-line impact; only 21% of companies have done it.

What it is

A quantified operating plan: scored assessment, opportunity portfolio on a return vs effort matrix, and a sequenced roadmap with a concrete first 90 days.

What it is not

A strategy deck, a technology audit, or a staffing pitch.

2 · Selling it

What the seller walks in with

The offer card

Six to eight weeks, fixed fee. Deliverables by name: readiness scorecard, opportunity portfolio on the return vs effort matrix, initiative outlines with build / buy / partner options, first-90-days plan, executive readout.

The sample report

An anonymized assessment report built from real Techjays engagements, actual process maps, target designs, and roadmaps with client identifiers removed. Answers the "show me what I get" buyer with work we have really done, not an invented example. Flagged as the next collateral investment.

The qualification profile

Mid-market, roughly 100 to 500 employees. Operations- or GTM-heavy. Board or CEO pressure on AI. Pilots that have not moved the P&L.

Three door-opener questions

  1. What did your last AI pilot change in the P&L?
  2. If your team doubled tomorrow at the same cost, where would you point it?
  3. Who in your company can draw how a customer order actually flows, end to end?

Designed so any answer opens the conversation, on ground where the buyer is the expert. For services buyers, adapt question 3 to "a customer request".

Objection answers, written down

"Why not a big firm?"Same study, a fraction of the fee, and a plan you can build rather than a strategy deck.
"You will just tell us to hire you."Fixed fee independent of any build decision; every recommendation carries build / buy / partner options; the client owns everything.
"Our data is not ready."Data readiness is one of the things the study assesses. It is not an entry ticket.
"We can do this in-house."Individual productivity, yes. Department-level re-architecture needs dedicated structure from outside the daily grind.

3 · Why Techjays

Three reasons clients choose us, each one verifiable

1

We build what we recommend

The estimates in the roadmap come from people who design and deliver these systems every week, so the plan is buildable, not theoretical. And because we can be asked to execute it, we have to live with our own advice: an incentive no pure-strategy firm has.

2

Engineering depth at a mid-market price

A comparable study from the big firms runs several times our fee and stops at strategy altitude. We work at workflow level: the actual steps, handoffs, and systems, because that is where the value hides and where our delivery teams operate daily.

3

Independence by design

Fixed fee, no obligation to build with us, every recommendation carries build / buy / partner options, and the client owns every artifact. If the right answer is their in-house team or another vendor, the report says so.

Proof

Delivery heritage across AI and data programs. Example outcome from prior consulting work: a three-week logistics process redesigned to run in three to four days. The proof section grows as the practice builds case studies; the first pilot engagement's case study slots in here.
