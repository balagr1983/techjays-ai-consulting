## Published dimension sets (what the market scores against)

| Framework | Owner | Dimensions / levels |
|-----------|-------|---------------------|
| AI Readiness Assessment | PwC | 12 enterprise domains; 5 maturity bands (AI-Vulnerable to AI-Driven Leader); 0-100 score |
| AI Adoption Maturity Model (Jun 2026) | Accenture + CMU SEI | 8 dimensions: organizational strategy, workforce and culture, workflow re-engineering, risk and governance, data, engineering, operations, ecosystem |
| AI Capability Maturity Assessment | KPMG Germany | 6 levels, Level 0 data readiness to Level 5 operationalisation at scale; GenAI-technique oriented |
| FOREST | Thoughtworks | 6 dimensions: Foundational architecture, Operating model, Ready data, Experiences for humans + AI, Strategic alignment, Trustworthy AI |
| Rewired (research base: 200+ at-scale AI transformations) | McKinsey | 6 dimensions: strategy, talent, operating model, technology, data, adoption and scaling *(extracted, unverified)* |
| Kriv AI readiness scorecard | Kriv AI | 7 dimensions + regulatory mapping (NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, sector rules) |
| EY.ai Maturity Model | EY | 7 dimensions, GenAI maturity; free 15-minute self-serve scan + report + roadmap |
| AI Deployment Matrix | Bain | 2x2 of deployment archetypes: Personal Productivity, Amplified Intelligence, Embedded Assistant, Digital Worker AI; diagnostic positions the client in the matrix |
| Agentic AI readiness assessment | Deloitte | 3 dimensions (embedded agentic capabilities, external-agent operability, trustworthiness); scoped to digital channels |
| Responsible AI Maturity Assessment (retired) | IBM | 9-question quiz scoring 3 themes (strategy and governance, data and technology, people and culture) |

**Convergence:** every framework scores 6 to 12 dimensions, and the recurring core is:
strategy, data, technology/engineering, talent/workforce, governance/risk, operating
model/workflow. A Techjays framework should cover this core, then differentiate on the
ROI-vs-cost stack-ranking output.

## Assessment methodology patterns (verified engagement structures)

- **Kriv AI (4 weeks):** W1 discovery (exec kickoff, 5 to 12 stakeholder interviews,
  artifact review, shadow-AI inventory) → W2 gap analysis with 1-5 scoring → W3
  target-state architecture + roadmap → W4 executive readout.
- **Storm Reply (6 weeks):** Explore & Understand → Diagnose & Assess → Shape & Design →
  Plan & Enable.
- **Slalom Azure assessment (4 to 6 weeks):** five workstreams: stakeholder alignment,
  current-state assessment, readiness/modernisation, business case, roadmap.
- **PwC:** leadership interviews scored per domain, benchmarked, converted to a
  prioritized 90-day / 6-month / 12-month action plan.

## What a credible report contains (the market-standard deliverable set)

1. Scored maturity / gap analysis against the named framework (per-dimension scores).
2. Benchmarking against peers or an industry compendium, where data exists.
3. Prioritized use-case portfolio: impact vs effort (PwC) or ROI vs cost (the Techjays
   2x2), stack-ranked, with quick wins flagged.
4. High-level target architecture views per priority use case.
5. ROI and cost estimates per initiative.
6. Time-phased roadmap (90-day / 6-month / 12-month is the common cut).
7. Governance / risk / responsible-AI section (increasingly expected; regulatory mapping
   where the sector demands it).
8. Executive summary (one page) + full report (Kriv's is 30 to 40 pages, a useful size
   anchor).

## Assessment dimensions the data says matter most (extracted, unverified)

From McKinsey's published State of AI survey research (Nov 2025 and Mar 2025 editions):
- **Workflow redesign** has the single biggest effect (of 25 attributes tested) on
  whether gen AI produces EBIT impact, yet only about 21% of adopters have fundamentally
  redesigned any workflow. An assessment that maps and redesigns workflows targets the
  highest-leverage gap. This is exactly the DND strength Techjays would rebrand.
- **CEO oversight of AI governance** is the element most correlated with bottom-line
  impact; only 28% of AI-using organizations have it.
- Fewer than 1 in 5 organizations track well-defined KPIs for gen AI solutions, the
  practice most correlated with EBIT impact. A KPI/measurement section belongs in the
  Techjays report template.
