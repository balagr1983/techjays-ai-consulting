# Peer Interview Questions - AI Consulting Intelligence

> For Bala's conversations with leadership-level peers who have implemented AI in their
> organizations. Goal: understand what pre-implementation consulting they bought, what
> they got, what it cost, and what they wished was different. Log answers per person in
> `peer-findings.md`.

## Before the AI work started

1. Before you implemented AI, did you run any kind of study, audit, or readiness
   assessment first? Was it internal or with an outside firm?
2. If an outside firm: who, and why that firm? Was cost, brand, or capability the
   deciding factor?
3. What did the engagement look like: how many weeks, how big a team, how much of your
   own people's time did it take?

## The deliverable

4. What did you actually receive at the end? (Report, roadmap, prioritized use-case
   list, business case, prototypes?)
5. What in that report did you actually use afterward, and what turned out to be
   shelf-ware?
6. Did it rank opportunities by ROI or cost? Did it get down to implementation-level
   detail, or stay at strategy level?

## Money and value

7. Roughly what did it cost? Did it feel worth it in hindsight?
8. If the same firm had offered to also build what they recommended, would that have
   been a plus (skin in the game) or a concern (biased advice)?

## The gap we want to fill

9. What do you wish someone had told you or mapped out before you started?
10. If a firm offered an 8-week deep-dive that maps your processes and hands you a
    stack-ranked, costed AI plan at a fraction of big-firm fees, what would make you
    trust them enough to sign?

## Hiring angle

11. Anyone in your network who does this kind of advisory work and might be open to a
    move?
