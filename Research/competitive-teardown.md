# Competitive Teardown - How AI Pre-Study Offerings Are Packaged

> Source: deep research sweeps, 17 Jul 2026 (pass 1) and 21 Jul 2026 (pass 2). All
> claims below marked "verified" survived 3-vote adversarial fact-checking against live
> primary sources (official firm pages, newsroom releases, marketplace listings).
> Offering pages change often; treat as current but perishable.

## The headline pattern (verified components, synthesized pattern)

Every AI pre-study offering examined is a **funnel into downstream delivery**. The tiers
monetize the top of the funnel differently:

| Tier | Monetization of the diagnostic | Example |
|------|-------------------------------|---------|
| Strategy firms | No standalone diagnostic SKU; a diagnostic opens the strategy engagement | McKinsey QuantumBlack; Bain (AI Deployment Matrix diagnostic) |
| Big Four | Either productize and charge for the diagnostic (PwC) or give a self-serve scored scan away as lead-gen (EY) | PwC AI Readiness Assessment; EY.ai Maturity Model |
| Mid-tier | Give it away as gated lead-gen, or fuse advisory + build into one offer | Thoughtworks FOREST; West Monroe |
| Boutique / cloud partners | Fixed-fee, fixed-duration (4 to 6 week) productized assessments with explicit deliverable lists | Kriv AI, Storm Reply |
| Tech-owned consulting | Productized advisory SKUs on cloud marketplaces (fixed scope, price via private offer) + free scored quiz funnels | IBM (Expert Labs Advise SKUs, retired RAI quiz, ICA4CT ladder) |

The common credible deliverable set across all tiers: **scored maturity/gap analysis
against a named multi-dimension framework (6 to 12 dimensions), benchmarking where data
exists, prioritized use-case portfolio with architecture views, ROI/cost estimates, and a
time-phased roadmap (typically 90-day / 6-month / 12-month)**.

## Firm-by-firm (verified)

### PwC - "AI Readiness Assessment" (the most productized Big Four diagnostic)
- Interview-based organizational diagnostic across **12 enterprise domains** (Strategic
  Vision, Data Governance, Talent, Risk, Business Model Resilience, etc.).
- Deliverables: a **0-100 readiness score** traceable to leadership responses, per-domain
  gap diagnosis, percentile benchmarking against a claimed 500+ organization compendium
  (self-reported figure), prioritized recommendations, time-bound action plan.
- Five-level maturity scale with named bands: AI-Vulnerable (0-29), AI-Aware (30-48),
  AI-Developing (49-66), AI-Advanced (67-83), AI-Driven Leader (84-100).
- **Funnel engineering is explicit:** recommendations classified Critical / High / Quick
  Win, scored by impact vs implementation effort; "PwC support is available on any item
  where deeper expertise is needed"; roadmap scores "update automatically as initiatives
  progress" (an ongoing engagement mechanism).
- Sources: pwc.com/us/en/tech-effect/ai-analytics/ai-readiness-assessment.html; pwc.com/us/en/services/ai/benchmarking.html

### Accenture + Carnegie Mellon SEI - "AI Adoption Maturity Model" (launched Jun 2026)
- **Eight dimensions:** organizational strategy, workforce and culture, workflow
  re-engineering, risk and governance, data, engineering, operations, ecosystem.
- Positioned as assessment-to-roadmap: baseline readiness, use-case identification,
  structured adoption roadmap, cross-industry benchmarking, feeding Accenture's
  "reinvention partner" delivery work.
- **The credibility play is the academic co-brand** (CMU SEI, originator of CMMI).
  Accenture CSO publicly criticizes competing models as "high-level strategy without
  engineering rigor". Same differentiation argument Techjays wants to make.
- Source: newsroom.accenture.com (8 Jun 2026 release), corroborated by CMU SEI.

### KPMG (Germany) - "AI Capability Maturity Assessment"
- White-paper-delivered framework, not a defined engagement (no stated phases, duration,
  or team shape).
- **Six maturity tiers**, Level 0 "Foundational Data Readiness" to Level 5
  "Operationalisation & Optimisation at Scale". Notably a technically grounded,
  GenAI-technique-oriented scale (prompting, contextualisation, reliability/control),
  not an org-wide one.
- Explicitly framed as the front end of downstream work ("structured basis for developing
  a targeted roadmap").
- Note: this is KPMG Germany's packaging; other member firms market separate offerings.

### McKinsey - QuantumBlack (no standalone diagnostic SKU)
- **Does NOT sell a discrete named AI readiness/maturity assessment.** Portfolio is four
  named lines: Artificial Intelligence, Data Transformation, Noble Intelligence, Digital
  Twins. Assessment work is folded into end-to-end transformation engagements.
- Positioning term: "hybrid intelligence" (people + AI).
- Advisor-who-also-builds via QuantumBlack Labs (internal tools/assets unit) and a partner
  ecosystem (e.g. NVIDIA) - even McKinsey does not position as sole-source builder.

### Slalom (mid-tier)
- No branded standalone readiness assessment product on its site; packages around named
  accelerators ("Set AI in Motion", "AI Accelerated Engineering", Agentforce Foundations
  Accelerator) plus self-serve tools (EnhanceIQ, Journey Navigator).
- **Marketplace listings disclose what websites omit:** a 2-day agentic AI workshop on AWS
  Marketplace; an Azure "Strategic Planning and Assessment" listed as a **4 to 6 week
  engagement staffed by a single Cloud Strategist**, five workstreams (stakeholder
  alignment, current-state assessment, readiness, business case, roadmap), price
  scope-dependent, **Microsoft partner funding eligible** (the vendor ecosystem can
  subsidize the pre-study).

### Thoughtworks - FOREST framework (free, lead-gen model)
- Branded proprietary framework assessing six dimensions: Foundational architecture,
  Operating model, Ready data, Experiences for humans + AI, Strategic alignment,
  Trustworthy AI.
- Deliverable is a personalized maturity report from a **gated self-serve online
  assessment** (contact details required): a top-of-funnel lead-generation instrument,
  not a scoped paid engagement.

### West Monroe (closest positioning analog to Techjays)
- **"We bring enterprise AI strategy and implementation together"** - the advisor also
  builds, with its Intellio Agent product framed as moving clients "from plan to
  production".
- Five service pillars: Agentic Transformation, AI-Enabled Workforce & Change Leadership,
  AI for M&A Value Creation, AI-Native Software Engineering, AI Strategy & Governance.
- This is the closest publicly documented builder-advisor positioning; verified as
  positioning only, not delivered capability.

### Deloitte - "agentic AI readiness assessment" (pass 2, 21 Jul)
- Framed in an executive thought-leadership piece ("An Executive's Framework for AI
  Capability Assessment", published 5 May 2026), **scoped to digital customer channels**,
  not general enterprise AI maturity.
- Three dimensions: embedded agentic capabilities, operability for external agents,
  trustworthiness (governance, compliance, observability, cost, performance,
  scalability, data provenance).
- Deliverables: quantified baseline, gap analysis, investment prioritization; explicit
  "From assessment to roadmap" funnel with named partner contacts.
- Caveat: a perspective page, not a contractual offering sheet. Notable that Deloitte's
  freshest assessment framing is **agentic-first** - the market is moving there.

### EY - "EY.ai Maturity Model" (pass 2, 21 Jul)
- **Free self-serve assessment, about 15 minutes**, scoring generative AI maturity
  across **seven dimensions**; deliverable is a "personal output report" plus a
  capability-development roadmap.
- Explicit handoff into paid EY consulting ("An EY team can also work with you...").
- The EY model = Thoughtworks' FOREST play at Big Four scale: free scored scan as
  lead-gen. Confirms the two Big Four postures: charge for depth (PwC) or give away
  breadth (EY).

### Bain - "AI Deployment Matrix" diagnostic (pass 2, 21 Jul)
- No named standalone readiness/maturity product. Packaging is a proprietary **2x2 "AI
  Deployment Matrix"** with four archetypes: Personal Productivity AI, Amplified
  Intelligence AI, Embedded Assistant AI, Digital Worker AI.
- Engagements "begin with a detailed diagnostic" positioning the client in the matrix,
  then an AI strategy built on use cases tied to concrete outcomes; downstream linkage
  via **buy/build/partner** sourcing guidance plus bespoke build by Bain's data science
  and AI engineering team.
- Team shape: blended industry + AI consultants, AI product managers, engineers,
  ecosystem partners.
- Note for us: Bain also leads with a 2x2 (deployment archetypes) and explicitly frames
  the buy/build/partner choice - the same objection-handling structure we drafted.

### IBM - three-layer productization (pass 2, 21 Jul)
- **Free scored quiz (retired):** the "AI Maturity Assessment" campaign page was a
  nine-question Responsible AI self-assessment scoring three themes, funneling into IBM
  Consulting's AI governance services and watsonx.governance. Retired between Jan and
  Jul 2026 (cite the Wayback snapshot if referenced).
- **Purchasable advisory SKUs on AWS Marketplace:** Technology Expert Labs "Advise"
  offerings (health checks, architecture guidance, optimization planning for watsonx
  and other IBM products), **priced per week of fixed-scope activities** - the clearest
  week-sized SKU packaging in the market. Product-attached, Expert Labs (not IBM
  Consulting), prices via private offer only. A companion "Expertise Connect"
  subscription (~1/4 FTE SME, 6/12-month terms) appears delisted.
- **Engagement ladder:** IBM Consulting's "Agentic Cloud Transformation Accelerator"
  (ICA4CT) on AWS Marketplace sells four explicit tiers: ~1-hour consult, 2 to 4 week
  PoC, 8 to 12 week MVP, 4 to 10 month delivery - self-described as "the front door for
  advisory-led engagements". Cloud transformation, not AI readiness, but the
  productized ladder is the pattern to copy.

### Kriv AI (boutique; the pricing datapoint)
- Fixed-fee "AI Readiness & Governance Assessment for Regulated Healthcare" on AWS
  Marketplace: **USD 15,000 to USD 25,000, 4-week virtual engagement.**
- Weekly cadence: W1 discovery (exec kickoff, 5 to 12 stakeholder interviews, artifact
  review, shadow-AI inventory); W2 gap analysis with 1-5 maturity scoring; W3 target-state
  architecture and roadmap; W4 executive readout.
- Deliverable: a **30 to 40 page report** - seven-dimension readiness scorecard,
  regulatory gap analysis mapped to named frameworks (HIPAA, NIST AI RMF, HITRUST,
  ISO/IEC 42001, state AI laws), reference architecture, risk-scored use-case inventory,
  12-month roadmap, policy templates, one-page executive summary.

### Storm Reply (mid-tier AWS specialist, part of Reply S.p.A.)
- "Data & AI Readiness Assessment", **about six weeks**, four phases: Explore &
  Understand, Diagnose & Assess, Shape & Design, Plan & Enable.
- Deliverables: maturity gap analysis, prioritized use-case portfolio with high-level
  architecture proposals, ROI analysis, cost estimates, adoption roadmap, upskilling path.
- Pricing private-offer only.

## Coverage gaps (honest limits after two passes)

- **BCG (BCG X) has produced no verifiable packaging claims in two consecutive passes.**
  Remaining ideas: BCG's published "AI Maturity Matrix" reports and any marketplace
  listings; low priority now that nine firms are covered.
- One claim was refuted and excluded in pass 1 (West Monroe's Intellio as a suite of
  eight named tools).
- Nearly all sources are the vendors' own marketing pages: correct and primary for
  packaging/positioning, but self-reported figures are unaudited and nothing verifies
  that any offering delivers as promised. Scope caveats to preserve when citing:
  Deloitte's assessment is digital-channels/agentic scoped; IBM's Advise SKUs are
  Expert Labs product-attached services, not IBM Consulting.
- Azure Marketplace listings for the big firms (vs AWS, covered for IBM) not yet swept.

## What this means for Techjays packaging (working take)

1. The market template Techjays should emulate is the **boutique/cloud-partner one**:
   fixed fee, fixed 4 to 6 week duration, explicit deliverable list, named framework with
   6 to 12 scored dimensions, ending in a stack-ranked, costed roadmap. This is exactly
   the shape of the offering Philip described, and it is a recognized, purchasable format.
2. A **named proprietary framework** is table stakes at every tier (PwC's 12 domains,
   Accenture's 8 dimensions, Thoughtworks' FOREST). Techjays needs one, with the 2x2
   ROI-vs-cost matrix as its signature output.
3. **The skin-in-the-game positioning is validated but not unique:** West Monroe already
   markets integrated strategy + build, and even McKinsey builds. The sharper Techjays
   claim is West Monroe-style integration at a mid-market price with fixed-bid follow-on.
4. **Accenture's move validates the engineering-rigor attack** on abstract strategy
   consulting - the same argument Philip makes. Expect this positioning to get crowded.
5. **Cloud marketplace listings (AWS/Azure) are a real distribution channel** for
   productized assessments, and partner funding (e.g. Microsoft) can subsidize the
   client's cost of the pre-study.
