# Research Plan - AI Consulting Practice Intelligence

> Owner: Bala, executed with Claude Code. Feeds the packaging decision and the setup
> roadmap for Philip. Source policy: official firm pages, analyst houses, trade press,
> public contract disclosures, peer interviews. No random blogs or social media posts.

## Track 1: Competitive teardown (how the offerings are packaged)

Firms: McKinsey (QuantumBlack), BCG (BCG X), Bain, Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG, Accenture,
IBM Consulting. Plus size-comparable firms (the real competitive set): Slalom,
Thoughtworks, West Monroe, and similar mid-tier / boutique AI consultancies.

For each firm capture:
- Offering name and terminology used (AI readiness assessment, AI maturity assessment, etc.)
- Structure: phases, duration, team shape
- Promised deliverables (report contents, roadmaps, business cases)
- How it links to downstream delivery (do they build, or hand off?)

Output: `competitive-teardown.md` (firm-by-firm matrix)

## Track 2: Pricing benchmarks

Sources: public-sector contract disclosures (US federal/state procurement records),
trade press (Consultancy.uk, Source Global Research), analyst commentary.
Every figure graded by confidence (disclosed contract > trade press > inference).

Output: `pricing-benchmarks.md`

## Track 3: Methodologies and frameworks

Published AI maturity models and assessment dimensions: Gartner, MIT Sloan + BCG AI
research, Deloitte State of AI, McKinsey Global AI Survey. Defines what a credible
assessment report contains; feeds our audit-report template design.

Output: `frameworks-and-methodologies.md`

## Track 4: Buyer-side demand signals

CEO/CIO survey data on AI adoption stalls and pre-study behavior (HBR, MIT SMR, annual
AI adoption surveys). Citable numbers for positioning and the sales deck.

Output: `buyer-demand-signals.md`

## Track 5: Peer interviews (Bala, offline)

Bala's leadership network: leaders who ran AI initiatives. Question set in
`peer-interview-questions.md`. Findings logged to `peer-findings.md` as they come in.

## Synthesis

All tracks roll up into: (1) a packaging recommendation for the Techjays offering,
(2) the practice setup roadmap for Philip (`Offering/roadmap.md`).
