Waites x Techjays

A structured approach to the agentic AI opportunity study

How we would map, quantify, and sequence the opportunities across sales, business development, marketing, and customer service, and hand over a roadmap the leadership team can execute.

Working draft v1 · for internal review
Part 1 · The objective
Where this starts

The objective set by Waites leadership

  • Double revenue per white-collar employee within about two years, by growing revenue with the current team rather than reducing it.
  • A coordinated, company-wide roadmap by end of 2026; the approach to get there is being finalized now.
  • Good AI initiative already exists in pockets. What is missing is a single quantified picture of where agentic AI creates the most leverage across the commercial and support functions.
Definition of done

The questions this study answers

  • Which processes, re-architected, move revenue per employee the most?
  • Where does agentic AI genuinely outperform simpler fixes, and where does it not?
  • What is each opportunity worth, and what would capturing it take?
  • In what order should Waites act, starting with the first 90 days?
  • Which data connections (NetSuite, HubSpot, internal systems) are needed along the way?
Part 2 · Approach
Operating principle

Three layers, applied in order

Each opportunity is taken through the layers in this order. AI is applied where it earns its place, not by default.

Boundaries

Scope

In scopeOut of scope
Sales, Business Development, Marketing, Customer Service, and the data connectivity underneath them (NetSuite, HubSpot, internal systems) Software development, which already has its own roadmap

The evaluation lens is revenue capacity: helping the same team produce more qualified pipeline, more closed revenue, and better retention. Cost effects are recorded where they appear.

Part 3 · Method
Department by department

The study cycle

  1. Understand. Structured interviews with leads and the people doing the work, supported by pre-shared questionnaires and observation.
  2. Map. As-is workflows: inputs, outputs, handoffs, systems touched, volumes, cycle times, and where the hours go.
  3. Re-architect. Target-state design through the three layers, including where humans stay in the loop.
  4. Quantify. Projected gain per opportunity, with assumptions stated and testable.
  5. Prioritize. All opportunities plotted on return vs effort and stack-ranked into a sequenced roadmap.
Prioritization

Return vs effort

Worked example

The business development function

A team of eight to ten people produces a target number of qualified meetings per month. The study maps how a meeting is actually made: list building, research, outreach, follow-ups, scheduling.

Each step is then classified: which parts a cleaner process removes, which are deterministic automation between HubSpot and the surrounding systems, and where agents working continuously and in multiple languages change the meeting economics.

The output is a costed target design with a projected meetings-per-person multiple, comparable against the other opportunities on the same matrix.

Part 4 · Engagement model
Working model

Hybrid: onsite and remote, by design

  • Onsite for the parts where presence matters most: re-architecture working sessions, observing the actual work, executive workshops, and the final readout.
  • Remote for what can be done efficiently over video calls, pre-shared questionnaires, and document review, completed before travel.
  • The onsite duration flexes with the pre-work: the more discovery completes remotely, the shorter the onsite window needs to be, which also reduces cost.
Team

Two roles, one onsite

RolePresenceResponsibility
Engagement leadOnsite, full-timeInterviews, workshops, process mapping, prioritization, executive readout

The time-zone offset works in the engagement's favor: material gathered onsite during the US working day is developed further overnight and is ready for review the next morning.

Commercial basis

How the engagement is priced

Professional fees on a USD 200 per hour basis. Travel and logistics for the onsite period are billed separately at actuals.

Indicative basis only. The final commercial construct depends on the scope, the team composition, and the onsite/remote split agreed once discovery boundaries are confirmed.

Pre-work sample

Business development questionnaire, excerpt

  1. Walk us through a lead's journey from first touch to a booked discovery call: each step, system, and handoff.
  2. What is the monthly meeting target per person, typical attainment, and where do the hours go in a normal week?
  3. Which outreach steps are templated or sequenced today, and which are crafted individually?
  4. What information is consulted before outreach, and what is missing?
  5. What are the main reasons a booked meeting does not become a qualified opportunity?

Full questionnaires for all four departments follow once scope is confirmed. Each is designed to be answered in under an hour.

Delivered through a working session with the executive team, in addition to the written report.

Working principles

How the advice stays useful

  • Every initiative carries build, buy, and partner options, including paths that do not involve Techjays. Where the in-house team is the right answer, the report says so.
  • Waites owns all artifacts produced during the engagement.
  • Effort and feasibility estimates come from a team that designs and builds these systems in delivery work, so the roadmap is grounded in implementation reality.
