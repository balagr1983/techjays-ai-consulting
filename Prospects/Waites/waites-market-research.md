# Waites - Pre-Pitch Market Research

> Deep research sweep, 28 Jul 2026 (104 agents, adversarially verified; 3 claims refuted
> and excluded). Purpose: sharpen the engagement-approach pitch. Confidence grades:
> HIGH = verified 3-0 against live primary sources; self-reported marketing metrics are
> flagged as such. Findings current to late Jul 2026.

## Track A: Financial and corporate footprint

**The headline: no verified financials exist in public sources.** No disclosed or
press-confirmed revenue, profit, growth rate, valuation, PE/debt event, UCC filing, or
litigation signal survived verification. The ~USD 50 million revenue and ~280 employee
figures (from Gerry) remain informal and unverified; treat as estimate-grade only and
never cite them back to the client.

What IS verified (all HIGH unless noted):

1. **New CRO with a revenue-growth mandate.** Ryan Bowman appointed Chief Revenue
   Officer (announced 20 May 2025, effective Apr 2025; previously CRO at K2view) "to
   lead the company's revenue growth strategy, supporting Waites' continued expansion".
   Still in seat as of mid-2026.
2. **Aggressive international expansion (Jun 2025).** New subsidiaries in Warsaw and
   Singapore; footprint ambitions across UK, Poland, Singapore, Japan, Australia, New
   Zealand, Latin America, Africa, Middle East. **Hybrid go-to-market:** direct sales in
   Europe/UK, technical distributors for ME/Africa/APAC (existing distributors in
   ANZ and Peru).
3. **Self-reported scale metrics** (MEDIUM; company-claimed, unaudited): 500,000+
   sensors across 5-6 continents; data points 10T+ (mid-2025) growing to 13T+ (Dec
   2025) with ~10 billion added daily; 99.92% defect detection coverage (no published
   methodology).

## Track B: Strategy signals from public statements

4. **The MaintainX partnership (8 Dec 2025) reveals their automation philosophy.**
   Waites anomaly detections auto-create "detailed, analyst-verified work orders" in
   MaintainX with two-way comment sync; goal stated as reducing MTTA/MTTR. Pattern:
   **automate the workflow AROUND the human analysts, keep the analysts in the
   verification loop.** This is exactly our three-layer philosophy applied by Waites
   themselves, in their product.
5. **They are doubling down on the human-analyst layer, not automating it away.**
   Official positioning through 2026: "proven AI, and real human expertise";
   "certified analyst-reviewed insights delivered in plain language"; every alert
   reviewed by a certified analyst before it reaches the customer; their own blog:
   "Why AI-Powered Predictive Maintenance Still Needs Humans".
6. **PiezoNode launch** extends monitoring into 250C+/480F+ environments (date
   ambiguity: announced Jun 2025 on Reliabilityweb, re-promoted Dec 2025 BusinessWire).

Not found despite targeting: Rob Ratterman's Forbes Council posts/interviews (nothing
survived verification), job-posting signals, commercial-model detail (subscription
economics). These remain open.

## Track C: Competitor AI positioning

7. **Augury (best-funded competitor):** USD 75 million Series F led by Lightrock,
   Feb 2025; USD 1,000 million+ valuation maintained from 2021 (not an up-round);
   USD 361 million total funding. Claims 99.9% failure-detection accuracy and 5-20x
   ROI (self-reported; near-mirror of Waites' 99.92%).
8. **Augury is still human-in-the-loop officially:** "Cat III and IV Vibration
   Analysts... validate every alert"; "AI accuracy and human expertise in the loop is
   what makes Machine Health prescriptive." A 2026 "AI Agents" launch automates
   post-diagnosis workflow (auto-creating CMMS work orders) - the automation frontier
   is moving TOWARD the diagnostic layer but not into it. (Cite cautiously: the AI
   Agents detail surfaced in verifier evidence, not as a standalone verified claim. A
   claim that Augury's CEO frames the next phase around agentic AI was REFUTED 0-3.)
9. **Fluke Reliability's Azima DLI is the compression evidence.** AI-first,
   human-exception model: the AI diagnostic engine handles **~93% of machine tests
   without human intervention**, with ISO CAT II-IV analysts handling only the complex
   ~7% (engine built on a 6,000+ rule expert system, 1,200+ fault types). Fluke
   advertises the automation rate as the selling point. The layer Waites staffs for
   every alert, a major competitor runs at 7% human touch.

**Not covered (no claims survived):** Siemens Senseye, SKF, Emerson, Nanoprecise,
Tractian, Erbessd, Aspen Mtell; and no verified evidence anywhere about competitors
using AI in their own commercial operations. Track C is a partial answer.

## What this means for the pitch (working view, to discuss)

- **The hook is the commercial scaling story, not the financials.** New CRO with a
  growth mandate + two new subsidiaries + a hybrid direct/distributor model across
  a dozen markets = a commercial function whose coordination load is exploding. That
  is precisely the surface our study addresses, and it is all public, citable fact.
- **Mirror their own philosophy back to them.** The MaintainX integration IS our
  three-layer approach in miniature: automate handoffs, preserve human judgment. Line
  for the deck: extend to sales, BD, marketing, and service the same pattern Waites
  already applied to maintenance execution.
- **Competitive urgency, used carefully.** Augury has a USD 361 million war chest;
  Azima runs the analyst layer at ~7% human touch. For Gerry/internal use this is
  strong urgency material; for the client version it needs diplomatic framing (their
  human-analyst differentiation is a strength worth defending with a more scalable
  commercial engine, not a mistake).
- **Do NOT use:** the USD 50 million revenue / 280 employee figures (unverified), any
  Ratterman personal AI stance beyond the public bio, Augury agentic-CEO framing
  (refuted).
